Laboratory Experiments

Ohm's Law Verification

Electricity

Use a resistor, voltmeter, ammeter, and variable DC supply to confirm the linear relationship between voltage and current.

Safety: Check circuit connections before powering. Use low voltage (below 12 V).

Simple Pendulum & Gravity

Mechanics

Determine the acceleration due to gravity (g) by measuring the period of a pendulum for different lengths.

Note: Keep angular displacement small (< 15°) for simple harmonic motion.

Refraction & Snell's Law

Optics

Trace a light ray through a rectangular glass block. Measure angles of incidence and refraction to verify Snell’s law.

Care: Handle glass block carefully; use a low-power ray box.
