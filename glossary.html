Physics Glossary

Acceleration

Rate of change of velocity (m/s²).

Amplitude

Maximum displacement from equilibrium in a wave.

Conduction

Transfer of heat through a solid by particle vibration.

Convection

Heat transfer in fluids via movement of the fluid itself.

Current (I)

Flow of electric charge, measured in amperes (A).

Electromagnet

A magnet created by electric current in a coil.

Frequency (f)

Number of complete waves per second, in hertz (Hz).

Inertia

Tendency of an object to resist changes in motion.

Ohm's Law

V = IR; voltage is proportional to current for a fixed resistor.

Period (T)

Time taken for one complete oscillation (seconds).

Potential difference

Energy per unit charge (voltage), in volts (V).

Radiation

Heat transfer via electromagnetic waves; no medium needed.

Resistance (R)

Opposition to current flow, measured in ohms (Ω).

Specific heat capacity

Energy required to raise 1 kg of a substance by 1 °C.

Velocity

Speed in a given direction (vector).

Wavelength (λ)

Distance between two consecutive crests or troughs.
