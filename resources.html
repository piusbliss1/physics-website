Core Physics Lessons

Electricity: Ohm's Law

Voltage, Current, Resistance

Ohm’s law relates potential difference (V), current (I), and resistance (R). Essential for analysing series and parallel circuits.

V = I × R

Series: Rtotal = R1 + R2 + ...
Parallel: 1/Rtotal = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + ...

Mechanics: Newton's Laws

Inertia, Force, Action-Reaction

Three fundamental laws that govern motion. From seatbelts to rocket propulsion, they explain everyday phenomena.

F = m × a

1st: Inertia. 2nd: Net force causes acceleration. 3rd: Forces come in pairs.

Waves: Properties

Transverse & Longitudinal

Waves transfer energy. Frequency (f), wavelength (λ), amplitude, and speed (v) are key parameters.

v = f × λ

Transverse: light, water ripples. Longitudinal: sound. Wave speed depends on medium.

Thermal Physics

Heat, Temperature & Transfer

Heat is energy in transit; temperature measures average kinetic energy. Conduction, convection, and radiation dominate transfer.

Q = m × c × ΔT

Specific heat capacity (c) determines energy needed to change temperature.
