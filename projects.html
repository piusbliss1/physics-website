Creative Physics Projects

Marble Roller Coaster

Mechanics & Energy

Construct a track from foam pipe insulation to explore potential and kinetic energy, friction, and loop dynamics.

Build: Use foam tubes, tape, and a marble. Include hills, loops, and measure speed at different points.

DIY Electromagnet

Magnetism & Electricity

Wind insulated copper wire around an iron nail and connect to a battery to create an electromagnet. Investigate factors affecting strength.

Test: Count how many paper clips it lifts. Vary number of coil turns or current (use resistor).

Solar Oven

Thermal Energy

Build a simple solar oven using a cardboard box, aluminium foil, and plastic wrap. Demonstrate the greenhouse effect and heat absorption.

Test: Place a thermometer inside and record temperature every 5 minutes. Try melting chocolate.
