About Physics in Action

Our Purpose

Physics in Action provides a structured, curriculum‑aligned resource for secondary school students and teachers. The website covers core theory, step‑by‑step laboratory practicals, and creative hands‑on projects. All content is designed to reinforce understanding through clear explanations and practical application.

How to Use

Navigate between the Teaching (concept lessons), Practical Work (laboratory experiments), and Projects (build‑based learning) modules via the top menu. Support pages like Glossary, Formulas, and Resources offer quick reference and further study.

Who It’s For

Created for learners aged 14–18, following GCSE, IGCSE, or equivalent secondary physics curricula. Also useful for teachers seeking ready‑made experiment instructions and project ideas.

Contact & Contributions

This is an open educational resource. To suggest improvements, report issues, or contribute additional content, please visit our GitHub repository.
